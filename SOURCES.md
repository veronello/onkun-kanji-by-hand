## Romaji verification workbooks

The following Excel workbooks document the romanization review process for the Kanji ON project.

- [OnKun_Romaji_Verified.xlsx](sources/OnKun_Romaji_Verified.xlsx) — Workbook containing the results of romaji verification.
- [OnKun_Romaji_Review.xlsx](sources/OnKun_Romaji_Review.xlsx) — Cases requiring additional review and verification.
- [OnKun_Romaji_Reviewed.xlsx](sources/OnKun_Romaji_Reviewed.xlsx) — Workbook containing the results of the subsequent review process.

These files are working materials for the project and may be updated as verification continues.


## Published card and exercise data

The card database is exported from the unchanged [OnKun_Romaji_Reviewed.xlsx](sources/OnKun_Romaji_Reviewed.xlsx). Column H supplies example romanization; column S governs its publication status. The original `Romaji Review` and `Romaji Audit` records are exported alongside the runtime data.

Reviewed Russian and English translations cover kanji 1–204 (913 source examples). Dictionary sources and sense notes are recorded in the [translation reports](docs/translations-181-204.md), with links to earlier batches. The published exercises use the shared card records and explicit reviewed IDs: [ON selection and exclusions](docs/on-drill-review.md), [KUN selection and exclusions](docs/kun-drill-review.md).
