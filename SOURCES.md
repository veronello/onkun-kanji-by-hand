## Romaji verification workbooks

The following Excel workbooks document the romanization review process for the Kanji ON project.

- [OnKun_Romaji_Verified.xlsx](sources/OnKun_Romaji_Verified.xlsx) — Workbook containing the results of romaji verification.
- [OnKun_Romaji_Review.xlsx](sources/OnKun_Romaji_Review.xlsx) — Cases requiring additional review and verification.
- [OnKun_Romaji_Reviewed.xlsx](sources/OnKun_Romaji_Reviewed.xlsx) — Workbook containing the results of the subsequent review process.

These files are working materials for the project and may be updated as verification continues.
