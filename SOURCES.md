## Reviewed romanization workbook (experimental branch)

[OnKun_Romaji_Reviewed.xlsx](sources/OnKun_Romaji_Reviewed.xlsx) — the user-designated version saved on 2026-10-13 at 12:28 UTC (14:28 Berlin). Copied byte-for-byte; not edited. Column H supplies example romanization; column S governs its publication status. The original `Romaji Review` and `Romaji Audit` records are exported alongside the runtime data.

[Translation sources and sense notes](docs/translations-12.md) document all 46 RU/EN example pairs for the first 12 kanji. These are concise editorial translations of meanings checked in Shōgakukan dictionaries (デジタル大辞泉, 精選版 日本国語大辞典, プログレッシブ和英中辞典), accessed via Kotobank and goo. No bulk translations were generated for the remaining records.
